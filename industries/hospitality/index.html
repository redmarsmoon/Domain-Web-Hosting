What is hospitality?

AI for hospitality covers enquiry and booking handling across the channels guests actually use, review response, and planning for demand that swings sharply by season, for hotels, lodges, camps and restaurants.

A hospitality enquiry has a short life. Somebody planning a trip is contacting several properties at once, and the one that answers first with a real answer frequently gets the booking.

Those enquiries arrive across WhatsApp, Instagram, email, booking platforms and the phone, and a property answering quickly on one channel and slowly on three is losing bookings it never sees.

Behind that sits a demand pattern that swings harder than most industries. A lodge in low season and the same lodge in August are different businesses, and staffing for the average serves neither.

What we measure before proposing anything

Duara works with hotels, safari lodges, camps and restaurant groups from Nairobi on enquiry handling, bookings and seasonal planning.

We measure response time by channel before proposing anything. Properties consistently believe they answer faster than they do, and the gap between the channel you watch and the ones you do not is usually where the lost bookings are.

Frequently asked questions about hospitality

What decides whether an enquiry converts?

Speed with a real answer. A guest contacting four properties books with whoever answers first with actual availability and a price.

Would an instant holding reply help?

Very little. An instant reply that does not answer the question is worth almost nothing, which is why acknowledgements do not improve conversion.

Which channels does it cover?

WhatsApp, Instagram, email, platform messages and phone together, because the unwatched channel is where bookings quietly go elsewhere.

How do we know our response times?

We measure them per channel first. Properties consistently believe they answer faster than they do.

Will it post review responses?

It drafts them. A public reply is your voice and reputation, so nothing goes out without a person approving the wording.

Can you write us positive reviews?

No. It is asked for occasionally and we decline, since it is dishonest and platforms detect it.

Can it quote availability?

Yes, from your property management system. Quoting availability you do not have creates a worse experience than answering slowly.

Will it prevent double bookings?

It consolidates channels into one availability view, which addresses the usual cause rather than the symptom.

Does it handle seasonal demand?

It makes your own patterns visible for staffing and stock, since planning for the average is wrong in both directions.

How long before enquiries are handled?

Four to eight weeks, and low season is the right time to implement.

Does it work for restaurants?

Yes, for reservations and enquiry handling, though the demand pattern is weekly rather than seasonal.

Can it handle group and corporate enquiries?

It gathers requirements and routes them to a person, since group pricing is a commercial judgement.

Will guests know they are not talking to staff?

Yes, and we recommend saying so. Guests mind far less than properties expect, provided the answer is useful.

Does it work in Swahili and other languages?

Yes, and for lodges and camps handling international guests, multiple languages are usually a requirement.

Can it upsell rooms or extras?

It can present options you have defined. Discounting and negotiation stay with your team.

Does it integrate with booking platforms?

Yes, with the common ones, which is where availability consistency problems usually originate.

What about deposits and payments?

It can send payment details and confirm receipt. Refund and dispute decisions stay with your staff.

Will it work for a small property?

Yes, and small properties often gain most since the owner is answering enquiries personally between other work.

Who can see guest contact details?

Only your own staff. Guest data stays in your systems and trains no public AI model.

What does a hospitality project cost?

Property count and enquiry volume determine it, quoted after the response review.

Glossary of terms

Hospitality terms in plain language.

Conversion
The proportion of enquiries that become bookings, which response speed affects most.
Channel
A route guests use to reach you, from WhatsApp to booking platforms.
Occupancy
The proportion of rooms or covers filled, which swings sharply by season.
Double booking
Two bookings for the same space, usually caused by channels not sharing availability.
Shoulder season
The period between peak and low, where pricing and staffing decisions matter most.
Response time
How long a guest waits for a real answer, measured per channel rather than overall.
