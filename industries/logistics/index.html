What is logistics?

AI for logistics covers clearing and freight documentation, demurrage risk, transporter coordination and cargo visibility for forwarders, clearing agents and transport operators moving goods through Kenyan ports and corridors.

In freight the expensive failures are documentary. A consignment sits at the port because a document is missing, incorrect or late, and demurrage accrues daily while somebody chases a signature.

The cost is entirely avoidable and entirely predictable. The document was always going to be needed, and the only question was whether it was prepared before or after the vessel arrived.

Around that sits transporter coordination, where a truck waiting is a truck not earning, and cargo visibility, where the client asking where their goods are is the single most common contact a forwarder receives.

Why we start with demurrage

Duara works with freight forwarders, clearing agents and transport operators from Nairobi and Mombasa on documentation, demurrage and coordination.

We start with demurrage because it is measurable in shillings per day. Most operators can name the last consignment that cost them and cannot say what documentation delays cost them last quarter in total, and that total is usually the argument.

Frequently asked questions about logistics

Why start with demurrage?

Because it is measurable in shillings per day and entirely predictable. The document was always going to be needed.

Do most operators know what it costs them?

They can name the last consignment that hurt and rarely the quarterly total. That total is usually the argument.

When should documents be chased?

From booking rather than arrival. Starting when the vessel is close removes all the slack and guarantees some charges.

Does it make customs declarations?

No. Declaration content and classification carry legal responsibility and stay with your licensed agents.

Will it advise on tariff classification?

No. Getting a classification wrong has consequences beyond cost, and it is specialist work we would not claim competence in.

Can it update clients on cargo status?

Yes, and status enquiries are the most common contact a forwarder receives, so this removes a large share of interruptions.

Does it help with transporter coordination?

Yes, scheduling against actual clearance progress rather than estimates, which is what keeps trucks earning instead of waiting.

Can it flag a consignment heading for trouble?

Yes, while there is still time. A warning once charges have started is a notification rather than a saving.

How long before documentation chasing improves?

Six to ten weeks, with early chasing going live first because demurrage savings fund everything after it.

Will it work with our clearing system?

Most common systems, feeding your existing records rather than creating a parallel set.

Can it handle cross border haulage?

Yes, and corridor documentation has its own requirements which we map alongside port clearance.

Does it track cargo location?

Where you have tracking feeds, yes, and where you do not, status against clearance milestones is usually what clients actually want.

Can it reduce truck waiting time?

Yes, through coordination against real clearance progress rather than optimistic scheduling.

Will it tell us when the delay was our fault?

Yes, and we report that honestly, since your own process is the part you can actually change.

Does it work for small forwarders?

Yes, and smaller operators frequently feel demurrage harder because a single bad consignment is a larger share of the month.

Can clients see status themselves?

Yes, through a link in the update, which reduces enquiries further.

What about document formats?

Documents arrive in every format including photographs, and the system reads them as they come rather than requiring clean scans.

Does it integrate with port systems?

Where interfaces exist, yes, and where they do not we work from your own records and are clear about the limits.

Who can see our client consignment data?

Only your own staff. Consignment records and client shipping details stay inside your systems, and no public AI model is trained on them.

What does a logistics project cost?

Consignment volume and integration count determine it, quoted after the demurrage review.

Glossary of terms

Freight terms in plain language.

Demurrage
Charges accruing daily when cargo is not cleared within the free period.
Clearing agent
The licensed party making customs declarations on an importer behalf.
Free period
The time allowed before demurrage begins, which documentation delay consumes.
CFS
A container freight station where cargo is held, stripped and released.
Consignment
A single shipment of goods, with its own documentation requirements.
Turnaround
How quickly a vehicle completes a job and becomes available again.
