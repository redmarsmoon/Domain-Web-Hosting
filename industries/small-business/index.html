AI for Small Business in Kenya

Most small businesses do not need us. This page explains when you might.

What is small business?

AI for small business means the narrow set of things that genuinely help an owner managed business, usually missed enquiries, quoting and invoice chasing, and an honest account of when a subscription tool or a changed process is the better answer.

A custom project has a floor cost. Below a certain size of problem, paying for one is worse value than a subscription tool, a cheap off the shelf product, or simply changing how something is done.

That is true of most small businesses most of the time, and saying so costs us work we would rather not take. A project that saves four hours a month will not repay itself, and the owner will conclude AI does not work when the real problem was that the answer was too big for the question.

Where it does help is narrower and more specific: enquiries missed because nobody could answer the phone, quotes that take days, and invoices that go unchased because chasing is uncomfortable.

When we tell you not to hire us

Duara works with owner managed businesses from Nairobi, and turns down more small business enquiries than we accept.

We will tell you to buy a subscription tool instead, or to change a process, when that is the better answer. It is the least profitable advice we give and it is the reason the clients we do take on are usually ones we can genuinely help.

Frequently asked questions about small business

Do small businesses actually need AI?

Most do not, and we say so. Below a certain size of problem a subscription tool or a changed process is better value than any project.

Why would you turn work down?

Because a project saving four hours a month cannot repay itself, and the owner ends up concluding AI does not work when the answer was simply too big.

What is the cost floor?

A custom build has a minimum cost regardless of how small the problem is. We state it early so nobody wastes time on a conversation that will not work.

What genuinely helps a small business?

Answering enquiries you cannot pick up, producing quotes faster, and chasing invoices consistently. Those three cover most of it.

Why are missed enquiries the priority?

Because the phone ringing while you are with a customer is the most common recoverable loss, and that enquiry goes to whoever answered theirs.

Can you recommend tools instead?

Yes, and we do it often. It is the least profitable advice we give and it is usually the right answer.

Will we depend on you afterwards?

No, and we design against it. A small business cannot carry a supplier dependency, so anything built has to run without us.

How much does a small project cost?

It depends on scope, and we tell you the floor before you invest time in the conversation rather than at the end of it.

Can it answer calls when I am busy?

Yes, capturing the enquiry details for you, which is usually the single most valuable thing for an owner managed business.

Will it help me quote faster?

Yes, from your own pricing. Speed decides more small contracts than price does, particularly in trades and services.

Is invoice chasing worth automating?

Often, because chasing is awkward when you know the customer personally, so consistent reminders collect better than occasional ones.

My customers only use WhatsApp, is that a problem?

No, it is the normal case. For most small businesses in Kenya that is where customers actually are rather than email or a website form.

What if I only have two staff?

Then be more sceptical, and so are we. At that size the honest answer is usually a tool or a process change.

Do I need a website first?

Not necessarily. Plenty of small businesses run entirely on WhatsApp and a phone, and we would not insist on a website you do not need.

Will you review whether it worked?

Yes, after a month, and we say so if it did not. The money involved matters more to you than to us.

Can I start with one thing?

You should. We keep small business scope to one problem rather than expanding it into a platform you cannot absorb.

What if my problem is just being disorganised?

Then software will not fix it and we will say so. Automating disorganisation makes it faster rather than better.

Do you work with informal businesses?

Yes, and the same honesty applies. Frequently the answer is a cheap tool and better habits rather than anything built.

Who sees my business information?

Only the people working on your project, and only for as long as the work runs.

What does a small business engagement cost?

The conversation is free and frequently ends in a recommendation not to proceed. Any build is quoted after that.

Glossary of terms

Terms used in this conversation.

Cost floor
The minimum a custom project costs, below which buying or changing is better value.
Subscription tool
An existing product paid for monthly, which frequently beats building.
Recoverable loss
Money currently lost that could realistically be recovered, such as a missed enquiry.
Owner bottleneck
Work that stops entirely when the owner is unavailable.
Payback
How long a change takes to repay what it cost, which should be short for a small business.
Scope
What a project covers, which for a small business should be one thing rather than several.
