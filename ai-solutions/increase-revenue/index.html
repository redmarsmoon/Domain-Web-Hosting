Increasing Revenue with AI

Recovering what you are already losing beats chasing what you never had.

Book a free strategy call Call 0723658888

What is increase revenue?

Increasing revenue with AI means recovering demand that already exists and is currently being lost, principally through unanswered enquiries, slow responses and forgotten repeat business, rather than creating demand that was not there.

AI does not create demand. It cannot make somebody want your product, and any supplier implying otherwise is selling something they cannot deliver.

What it can do is stop you losing demand you already have. An enquiry nobody answered, a quote that arrived after the customer had bought elsewhere, a repeat order nobody was reminded to ask for. That revenue existed and went somewhere else.

This is a smaller claim than the market usually makes and it is a far more reliable one, because the demand is already demonstrated. You are not betting on interest existing; you are recovering interest that was already expressed.

What we will not claim

Duara works with businesses in Kenya on recovering demand that is currently being lost rather than on generating new demand.

We will not claim AI increases demand. Where a client needs more customers rather than better handling of the ones contacting them, the answer is sales and marketing effort, and we say so rather than selling a system that cannot produce what they need.

Frequently asked questions about increase revenue

Can AI increase our sales?

It can recover sales you are losing. It cannot make somebody want your product, and any supplier implying otherwise is overselling.

Why is recovery more reliable than generating demand?

Because the demand already exists. Somebody who contacted you wants something, so you are not betting on interest existing.

Where is the revenue actually being lost?

Unanswered enquiries, responses that arrive after the decision, and repeat business nobody was reminded to chase.

Does response speed really decide sales?

In competitive situations, usually. The first credible response frequently wins, and that is a mechanical problem rather than a commercial one.

What if we simply need more customers?

Then we say so. A business with too few enquiries needs sales and marketing effort, and no system we build produces customers.

How do you measure recovered revenue?

Against a recorded baseline of unanswered enquiries and response times, counted before anything changes.

Would some of those orders have arrived anyway?

Some would, and we state attribution conservatively rather than claiming every conversion as recovered.

Why is repeat business the largest gain?

Because it is the cheapest revenue any business has, and it is routinely lost to nothing more than nobody asking.

How quickly does this show results?

From the next enquiry, which is faster than a marketing cycle. Repeat business gains follow the customer own reorder rhythm.

Do you count lost enquiries first?

Yes, and it is usually the first time that number has existed. Occasionally it shows the problem is smaller than assumed.

Can it work over WhatsApp and social channels?

Yes, and in Kenya that is frequently where the unanswered enquiries actually are rather than on email.

Will it push customers to buy more?

It prompts on their own cycle rather than pressuring. Aggressive selling into a customer relationship costs more than it returns.

Can it help with pricing?

Pricing is a commercial judgement we do not automate. Better response speed affects more sales than a price change usually does.

What about upselling?

It can present options you have defined. Whether to discount or negotiate stays with your people.

Is this different from marketing automation?

Yes. That runs campaigns to a list. This recovers individual enquiries and orders that were already in motion.

Does it work for a service business?

Yes, and quote speed usually matters more in services than in products, since the buyer is comparing availability as well as price.

How long before we see the effect?

Four to seven weeks to implement, with the first recovered enquiries typically in the first week of running.

What if our conversion is already good?

Then the gain is smaller and we would say so at the review rather than after the build.

Who sees our enquiry and customer data?

Only your own staff. Enquiry records and customer history stay inside your systems.

What does revenue recovery work cost?

Channel count and enquiry volume determine it, quoted after the demand review.

Glossary of terms

Revenue terms in plain language.

Expressed demand
Somebody who has already contacted you, as distinct from a prospect who has not.
Lost enquiry
A contact that was never answered, which is rarely counted anywhere.
Response time
How long a customer waits, which decides most competitive situations before price does.
Repeat cycle
How often a particular customer normally reorders, which differs by customer.
Attribution
Tracing a sale back to what caused it, stated conservatively where the link is uncertain.
Recovered revenue
Sales that would have been lost, as distinct from new demand created.
