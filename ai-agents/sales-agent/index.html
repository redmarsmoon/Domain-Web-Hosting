Sales Agent

Works your leads consistently, including the ones your team never gets round to.

What is sales agent?

A sales agent is software that works your lead list the same way every time: contacting new enquiries promptly, asking the qualifying questions your team would ask, booking meetings where they are warranted, and updating the pipeline from what actually happened.

Leads go cold because following up is repetitive and easy to postpone. The first two contacts happen, the fifth almost never does, and the deals that close on the fifth are simply lost.

The problem is not effort. Salespeople postpone follow up because a live conversation always outranks a scheduled one, which is rational for them and expensive for the business.

An agent works the list identically regardless of how the week is going. It contacts new leads, records what it learns, books meetings for those worth meeting, and keeps following the rest at a sensible pace.

Problems this solves

These four describe a sales function losing deals to process rather than to competitors.

  • error Follow up that stops at two contacts. Most deals need several touches and most teams manage two, so the ones that close later never get the chance.
  • error Older leads never worked. Enquiries from three months ago sit untouched because new ones always feel more promising, which is usually wrong.
  • error A pipeline built from memory. The CRM reflects what somebody typed up on Friday rather than what happened during the week.
  • error Inconsistent qualification. Different salespeople ask different questions, so leads arrive at meetings with wildly varying amounts known about them.

Features

  • tune Early handover by design. The agent passes a warm prospect to a person quickly rather than holding the conversation to appear capable.
  • tune The whole list, not just the new leads. Older enquiries are worked on a slower cadence, which is where a meaningful share of recoverable revenue sits.
  • tune Consistent qualification. Every lead is asked the same questions, so meetings start from a comparable base rather than varying by salesperson.
  • tune Capped and stopping sequences. Follow up is limited and halts immediately on any reply, because chasing somebody who has answered is the fastest way to lose them.
  • tune Pipeline written from activity. Records reflect what happened rather than what somebody reconstructed at the end of the week.

Benefits

  • trending_up Deals that needed five touches get five. Consistency recovers the deals that were always winnable but never followed through.
  • trending_up Dormant leads become pipeline. The back catalogue of old enquiries gets worked, which usually produces meetings nobody expected.
  • trending_up Salespeople spend time in conversations. The admin and the early contact are handled, so their time goes to prospects who are actually engaged.
  • trending_up Forecasting improves. A pipeline reflecting reality produces a forecast that survives contact with the month end.

Technologies we use

  • memory CRM integration. A live link into your pipeline records, so lead status and activity update as the agent works rather than afterwards.
  • memory Multi channel contact. Email, WhatsApp and SMS, using whichever channel the lead originally arrived through.
  • memory Qualification logic. The branching questions your team uses, so answers arrive structured rather than as free text.
  • memory Calendar booking. Live availability from your sales calendars so meetings are offered accurately.

Handover timing is the design decision that matters. Too late and the prospect cools; too early and your team drowns in unqualified conversations.

How the process works, step by step

A sales agent is usually working live leads within four to five weeks, beginning with older leads rather than new ones.

  1. Step 1

    Free pipeline review

    We look at how many leads are worked, how many touches each receives, and how many are never contacted at all.

  2. Step 2

    Qualification capture

    We record the questions your strongest salesperson asks, because those are the ones worth making consistent.

  3. Step 3

    Old leads first

    The agent starts on dormant leads, where the downside of a clumsy contact is lowest and the upside is real.

  4. Step 4

    New leads added

    Once wording and handover timing are proven on old leads, it takes new enquiries as well.

  5. Step 5

    Handover tuning

    The engagement threshold is adjusted against what your salespeople consider a genuinely warm prospect.

Frequently asked questions about sales agent

How is this different from an AI sales assistant?

The assistant responds to inbound enquiries immediately. This agent works the whole list over time, including old leads nobody has touched.

When does it hand over to a person?

Early, as soon as a prospect is genuinely engaged. An agent that holds the conversation to appear capable loses the deal.

Why start with old leads?

Because a clumsy contact costs least there and the upside is real. Dormant enquiries are usually still contactable and nobody is working them.

Will it annoy prospects?

Follow up is capped from evidence, halts on any reply, and uses wording your salespeople approved. We decline requests to raise the caps.

Does it stop chasing when somebody replies?

Immediately. Continuing to chase a prospect who has responded is the most common and most damaging failure in automated selling.

Which CRMs does it work with?

HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho and most others through their API, which we confirm during the pipeline review.

What questions does it ask?

The ones your strongest salesperson asks. We capture those rather than importing a generic qualification script.

Can it book meetings?

Yes, from live calendar availability, so it only offers times that genuinely exist.

Which channels does it use?

Email, WhatsApp and SMS, defaulting to whichever channel the lead originally came through.

Will prospects know it is automated?

We recommend disclosure. Discovering it during a sales conversation damages trust at precisely the wrong moment.

How long before it is working leads?

Four to five weeks, starting on dormant leads before it touches new enquiries.

Does it replace salespeople?

No. It handles early contact and admin so your team spends time in conversations with engaged prospects.

What happens to leads it disqualifies?

They go to a slower nurture cadence rather than being discarded, because timing rather than fit is usually the reason.

Can it handle Swahili?

Yes, including mixed language messages, which are common in written enquiries here.

How do we know it is working?

Contacts per lead, meetings booked and conversion, measured against the baseline from the pipeline review.

Does it update the CRM?

Yes, from actual activity rather than from anybody typing it up later, which is what makes the pipeline trustworthy.

What if it books a poor meeting?

The handover threshold is tuned against what your salespeople consider warm, and adjusting it upward is normal in the first month.

Can we exclude certain leads?

Yes, by source, value or account, so referrals or key accounts go straight to a person if you prefer.

Is lead data used for training?

No. It stays in your CRM and is never used to train public AI models.

What does it cost to run?

Lead volume and message counts set it, quoted from your own pipeline figures before any build starts.

Glossary of terms

Sales agent terms in plain language.

Lead
An enquiry that has not yet been qualified as worth pursuing.
Qualification
Establishing whether a lead is worth a meeting and what that meeting needs to cover.
Cadence
The rhythm of follow up contacts, including how many and how far apart.
Handover
Passing a prospect from the agent to a salesperson, ideally while they are actively engaged.
Dormant lead
An older enquiry that was never worked to conclusion and is usually still contactable.
Engagement signal
Behaviour indicating genuine interest, such as replying, opening repeatedly or asking a question.
