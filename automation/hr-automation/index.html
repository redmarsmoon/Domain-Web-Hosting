HR Automation

Leave, payroll inputs and statutory filing prepared without the spreadsheets.

What is HR automation?

HR automation handles the calculation and administration work in a personnel function, including leave accrual and balances, payroll inputs, statutory deduction preparation, contract generation and onboarding checklists, leaving every decision about a person to your HR team.

HR administration is arithmetic and deadlines. Leave accrues at a rate, balances carry over under a rule, payroll inputs are due on a date, and statutory deductions must be filed whether or not anybody had time.

Most Kenyan employers run this on spreadsheets. It works until somebody disputes a leave balance, or the person who built the payroll workbook leaves, or a filing deadline arrives during a busy month.

None of this requires judgement. It requires the same calculation applied consistently and a deadline that does not depend on memory.

What we automate and what we never will

Duara automates HR administration from Nairobi, built around Kenyan statutory requirements including PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the housing levy rather than adapted from a system designed elsewhere.

We automate calculation and never decisions about people. Nothing we build ends employment, issues a warning or scores performance, because those are legal and human acts that need a person who can be held accountable for them.

Problems this solves

These are calculation and deadline problems, which is the safest category of HR work to automate.

  • error Leave balances that get disputed. Accrual sits in a spreadsheet only one person understands, so a disagreement has no authoritative answer.
  • error Payroll inputs compiled by hand. Overtime, absence and allowances are gathered manually every month, which is slow and error prone at exactly the wrong time.
  • error Statutory deadlines under pressure. PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and housing levy submissions are prepared in a rush because nothing prompts them earlier.
  • error Onboarding steps missed. A new starter reaches their second week without a contract signed or a system account, because the checklist was informal.

Frequently asked questions about HR automation

Does it run our payroll?

It prepares the inputs and statutory schedules. Running payroll itself stays with your payroll system or provider.

Does it handle Kenyan statutory deductions?

Yes, PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the housing levy, built for those requirements rather than adapted from a foreign system.

What happens when the rates change?

The rules are updated. We build them to be maintained rather than embedded, because no system stays correct by itself.

Do you advise on tax treatment?

No. We build the calculation and your payroll or tax adviser confirms the treatment. That distinction matters.

Can it terminate employment or issue warnings?

No, and we will not build it. Those are legal and human acts requiring a person who can be held accountable.

Does it score staff performance?

No. We automate calculation and administration, never judgements about people.

How does it handle leave carryover?

By your written rules, which we document first because two managers frequently give different answers today.

What about our existing leave balances?

Reconciled and agreed before automation starts. Inheriting a disputed balance simply automates the dispute.

Can staff check their own leave?

Yes, and self service removes most disputes, since disagreements are usually about visibility rather than the number.

Does it work with our payroll software?

Yes, feeding inputs into common Kenyan and international payroll systems rather than replacing them.

Can it generate employment contracts?

Yes, from your approved templates. The wording is yours and should be reviewed by your lawyer.

Does it manage onboarding?

It drives the checklist to completion with reminders, so a new starter is not in week two without a contract.

How long before leave is running on it?

Five to eight weeks, starting with documenting leave rules that are often applied inconsistently today.

Can it handle shift and casual workers?

Yes, and these are usually where manual calculation causes the most errors and disputes.

Does it track overtime?

Yes, from attendance records, applying your own overtime rules rather than an assumed standard.

What about staff without smartphones?

Self service is optional. Balances can be issued on payslips or through a supervisor where access is limited.

Will it flag statutory deadlines?

Yes, ahead of time rather than on the day, which is the main reason filing stops being a scramble.

Can it handle several locations?

Yes, including different terms by site where those genuinely differ.

Is our staff data confidential?

Yes. Personnel records stay in your systems, are not exported to us, and no public AI model is trained on them.

What does HR automation cost?

Headcount and the number of processes determine it, quoted after the HR review.

Glossary of terms

Kenyan HR administration terms.

Accrual
The rate at which leave entitlement builds up over a period of service.
Carryover
Unused leave permitted to move into the following period, under rules that vary by employer.
PAYE
Pay As You Earn, the income tax deducted from salaries and remitted to KRA.
NSSF
The National Social Security Fund, a statutory pension contribution deducted from pay.
SHIF
The Social Health Insurance Fund, the statutory health deduction administered by the Social Health Authority.
Payroll input
The overtime, absence and allowance data payroll needs before it can be run.
