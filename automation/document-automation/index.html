Document Automation

Documents built from your data, so the wrong version stops going out.

Book a free strategy call Call 0723658888

What is document automation?

Document automation generates contracts, quotes, invoices, letters and reports by filling approved templates with data from your systems, removing the copy and paste step and the version confusion that comes with it.

The usual method is opening the last similar document and editing it. It is quick, everybody does it, and it is how a client name from a previous deal ends up in a contract.

It also means nobody knows which version is current. Four slightly different quote templates circulate, two of them contain payment terms your finance team stopped accepting last year, and you find out when a client holds you to them.

Generating from a controlled template and live data removes both failures at once. The document is correct because the data is correct, and there is only one template.

Why nothing leaves without approval

Duara builds document automation from Nairobi for organisations producing the same documents repeatedly, where the current method is editing a copy of the last one.

We keep an approval step before anything leaves the building. Generating a contract in four seconds is only an improvement if somebody still looks at it, and clients who remove that step to save the click are the ones who later send a client the wrong payment terms at speed.

Frequently asked questions about document automation

What does this actually replace?

The habit of opening the last similar document and editing it, which is how a previous client name ends up in a live contract.

Can it generate contracts?

Yes, from your approved templates with conditional clauses. The legal wording stays yours and should be reviewed by your lawyer.

Do you write the terms for us?

No. We build the mechanism, not the legal content. Template wording is a decision for you and your advisers.

Can documents be sent automatically?

They can, and we advise against it. Generating in seconds only helps if somebody still checks before it leaves.

Why keep a manual approval step?

Because clients who remove it to save a click are the ones who later send the wrong payment terms at speed.

Where does the data come from?

Your CRM, ERP or database, so names and figures are pulled from source rather than copied from a previous document.

How many templates can we have?

As many as you genuinely need, though consolidation usually reduces the number considerably.

What if our templates contradict each other?

We report the contradictions rather than picking one. Finding terms nobody meant to still be offering is a common outcome.

Can clauses vary by deal?

Yes, through conditional logic, so a section appears only where it applies rather than being deleted manually.

What formats does it output?

PDF normally, with editable formats where a document genuinely needs further work.

How long before documents generate themselves?

Three to six weeks per document family, with template design taking longer than the software.

Why does template design take so long?

Turning a document people have edited for years into clean conditional logic is genuinely the hard part.

Does it work with our document storage?

Yes, filing output to SharePoint, Google Drive or your own system with the template version recorded.

Can we still edit a generated document?

Yes, and edits before approval are normal. What changes is that you start from the correct base.

Does it handle signatures?

It integrates with e-signature tools where you use them, though the choice of provider is yours.

Can it produce documents in Swahili?

Yes, where you supply the template in Swahili. We do not translate legal wording.

Who can generate which documents?

Permissions follow your own rules, so a junior cannot issue a document that requires senior sign off.

What if a template needs changing?

It is updated centrally and the old version withdrawn, so the change takes effect everywhere at once.

Are our documents confidential?

Yes. Templates and generated documents stay in your systems and no public AI model is trained on them.

What does document automation cost?

The number of document families and template complexity determine it, quoted after the document review.

Glossary of terms

Document terms in plain language.

Template
The controlled master document that generated documents are produced from.
Merge field
A placeholder filled with a value from your data when the document is generated.
Conditional clause
A section that appears only when defined conditions are met.
Version control
Ensuring only the current approved template is in use.
Document trail
The record of what was generated, from what, by whom and when.
Document family
A group of related documents sharing structure, such as all quote types.
